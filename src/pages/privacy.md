**Alarma** is built around a simple principle: your voice belongs to you. This page explains what we store, where, and why.

## What stays on your device

Everything you create in Alarma — your alarms, voice clips, and library — is stored locally on your iPhone. There's no account and no sign-in, and your voice clips are never uploaded to our servers or shared with anyone.

## Third-party services we rely on

We keep this list short on purpose. Each service below receives only what it needs to do its job, and none of it is linked to your voice clips.

**RevenueCat** — Alarma uses RevenueCat to process subscription purchases through the App Store and to collect anonymous metrics about purchases and revenue. This information is anonymous and cannot be linked back to an individual user. See the [RevenueCat privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

**TelemetryDeck** — We use TelemetryDeck to understand how the app is used in aggregate — how often features are opened, which screens are visited, that kind of thing. TelemetryDeck is built for privacy: it only receives anonymous, salted signals and never identifies you personally. See the [TelemetryDeck privacy policy](https://telemetrydeck.com/privacy).

**Sentry** — We use Sentry to receive crash reports and error diagnostics so we can fix bugs quickly. Sentry receives technical information about the crash (stack trace, device model, OS version) but nothing about your voice clips. See the [Sentry privacy policy](https://sentry.io/privacy/).

**WinWinKit** — Alarma's affiliate program for creators runs on WinWinKit. If you join the program, the details you provide when applying and receiving payouts are handled by WinWinKit. See the [WinWinKit privacy policy](https://winwinkit.com/privacy/).

## What we don't do

- We don't sell or share your data with advertisers or third parties.
- We don't track you across other apps or websites.
- We don't collect, upload, or analyze your voice clips.

## Your choices

Your voice clips live only on your device, so you're always in control. You can delete any clip from inside the app, and deleting Alarma removes all of its data from your iPhone.

## Your rights and governing law

This policy is governed by Danish law, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Danish Data Protection Act. Under these rules you have the right to access, correct, and delete personal data about you, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

If you believe we have handled your data incorrectly, you can file a complaint with the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet) at [datatilsynet.dk](https://www.datatilsynet.dk).
