Welcome to the Alarma affiliate program — we're glad you're here. This playbook covers what Alarma is, how to talk about it, and a few ideas that tend to work well. Use it as a starting point and make it your own: your audience knows your voice best.

## Alarma in one sentence

**Alarma lets you wake up to the voices of the people you love instead of a beep.** You record a voice — a partner, a parent, a kid, a friend, or yourself — and set it as your alarm.

## How it works

1. **Get the app** from the App Store.
2. **Record a voice** — hand your phone to someone you love and let them record a wake-up message.
3. **Set the alarm** and wake up to that voice.

Everything stays on the iPhone. There's no account and no sign-in.

## Your referral code

Your personal referral code is in your WinWinKit dashboard. Include it every time you mention Alarma — in your caption, on screen, and out loud in videos — so your audience's purchases are credited to you. You earn **50% of the profit** from the users you refer, tracked and paid out through WinWinKit.

## Content ideas

- **The wake-up reaction.** Secretly record a message on your partner's or roommate's phone and film them waking up to it.
- **Morning routine.** Show Alarma as the first moment of your day — your alarm is a voice, not a siren.
- **Before a trip.** Have a partner, parent, or friend record a message before you leave, and wake up to their voice while you're apart.
- **For kids.** A parent records a gentle wake-up for their child — or the kid records one for the parent.
- **Beep vs. voice.** A quick side-by-side: the standard alarm sound, then a loved one's voice.
- **Record your future self.** Leave yourself a motivating message for an important morning.

## Hooks you can borrow

- "I haven't heard my alarm beep in weeks — this is what wakes me up now."
- "POV: your mom recorded your alarm before you moved out."
- "I recorded a wake-up message on my boyfriend's phone. Watch."
- "The nicest way to wake up, and it took 10 seconds to set up."

## Do

- **Show the app on screen** — recording a voice and the alarm going off are the most convincing moments.
- **Be genuine.** Share why you'd actually use it.
- **Disclose the partnership** clearly, following the rules of your platform and country (for example, #ad or a paid-partnership label).
- **Always include your referral code.**

## Don't

- **Don't promise features Alarma doesn't have.** Recordings stay on the device — there's no account, cloud sync, or sharing between users.
- **Don't record or publish someone's voice without their permission.**
- **Don't use misleading claims** about pricing, discounts, or results.
- **Don't spam** comments, DMs, or communities with your code.
- **Don't run paid ads on the Alarma name** or impersonate Alarma's official accounts.
